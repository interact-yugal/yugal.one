Collab for Chrome—a lightweight extension to effortlessly print AppSheet views and subtly enhance the AppSheet UI with Inter font.

Add to Chrome

Features

  • Flexible print triggers: Print a report directly from your app by hitting the native “Cmd+P” or “Ctrl+P” or via a dedicated print action built into the extension's popup.
  • Professional layout: Get your app's branding, view name and print timestamp right at the top.
  • Native grouping & filtering: Seamlessly capture your active view, including search filters, column filters and grouped headers with their calculated aggregates.
  • Modern typography: Subtly enhance your AppSheet app's UI with the clean and minimalist Inter font.
  • 100% local & secure: Process your data directly within your browser's active tab, ensuring your data is never transmitted to external servers.
