Features

  • Auto-sync: Keep your data up to date by turning on auto-sync and selecting a sync interval of your choice.
  • Sleek typography: Elevate your interface with a simple switch that applies the Inter font across the system, delivering a clean and minimalist visual experience.
  • Seamless printing: Effortlessly print your table and deck views that preserve all filters, groupings, and their corresponding aggregates, while seamlessly embedding your branding, title and metadata.
  • 100% local & secure: Process the data directly within your browser, ensuring your data is never transmitted to external servers.
