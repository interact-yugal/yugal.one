Print

Introducing Collab Print—a lightweight Chrome extension that integrates directly with your AppSheet app.

Printing AppSheet views is finally effortless.

Features

  • Native print integration: Prepares a minimalist report directly from your app whenever you hit Cmd+P or Ctrl+P.
  • Professional layout: Injects the app name, app logo, view name, and print timestamp right at the top.
  • Native grouping & filtering: Captures active filters, searches, groupings, and aggregates directly from your current view.
  • 100% local & secure: Processes all data strictly within your browser without ever transmitting your private information to external servers.
